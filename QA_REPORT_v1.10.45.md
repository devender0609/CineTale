# CineTale v1.10.45 — Self-Healing Production Logic RC

## Scope
This release makes the production-logic gate self-healing for deterministic pre-generation defects. CineTale now attempts a safe repair before any billable media generation, then validates again. If a conflict cannot be repaired safely, production remains blocked and the Studio shows the exact reason instead of a generic warning.

## New behavior
- Scene dialogue remains authoritative for speaker ownership and spoken lines.
- Ungenerated shot plans automatically repair speaker mismatches, spoken-line mismatches, dialogue-count mismatches, visual-only dialogue metadata, duplicate shot IDs/orders, timeline overlaps, and exact duplicate visual beats when the correction is deterministic.
- Stable dialogue character IDs are honored when checking whether a speaking shot maps to a real locked cast identity.
- The repaired plan is revalidated before it is shown as verified.
- The Studio shows a concise reason beneath `Plan needs attention` when an unresolved conflict remains.
- A `Recheck & repair` action is available as a fallback for ungenerated scenes.
- Once durable/paid shot media exists, CineTale fails closed and does not silently reshuffle shot ownership or order.
- Manual shot generation, automatic scene/episode production, and one-click final production continue to use the same logic gate.

## Credit/media safety
- Self-healing is limited to scenes without produced shot media.
- Existing durable video or synchronized dialogue assets are never silently reassigned or regenerated by the repair pass.
- Unresolved ambiguity blocks generation before provider submission.

## Live-provider limitation
No new billable Veo, ElevenLabs, image-generation, or lip-sync call was intentionally made during packaging QA. Provider behavior still requires a deployed-browser verification.
