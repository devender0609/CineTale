# CineTale v1.10.32 — Shot Navigation & Voice Preview RC

## Scope
- Story shot cards are clickable production navigation.
- Shot 1 is the default selected shot for every scene.
- Selecting a READY shot routes the large preview to that exact shot.
- Selecting a PLANNED/RECREATE shot shows truthful no-media state and a shot-specific generation action.
- READY shots cannot be regenerated accidentally from the primary action.
- Scene `Listen` previews dialogue only when dialogue exists, so narrator audio cannot play before a selected character voice.
- Character Voice modal X is an explicit button; X, backdrop, and Escape close the modal.
- Existing durable source/synchronized media remain authoritative and are not regenerated by selection.
